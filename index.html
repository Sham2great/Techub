01 / Introduction

What is NEDCO?

NEDCO (National Entrepreneurship Development Company Limited) is a wholly state-owned enterprise (limited liability company) and microfinance development agency in Trinidad and Tobago.

About the organisation

It is completely owned by the Government of Trinidad and Tobago and operates under the oversight of the Ministry of Youth Development and National Service.

As a non-bank microfinance institution, NEDCO provides small loans to micro and small business owners who may have difficulty accessing financing from commercial banks, including entrepreneurs who may not meet traditional collateral requirements.

In addition to financing, NEDCO supports entrepreneurs through business training, mentorship and advisory services to help micro and small enterprises develop, grow and succeed.

ESTABLISHEDAugust 2002

NEDCO was established by the Government of Trinidad and Tobago as a state-owned, limited liability company to support the development of micro and small enterprises.

Why it was established

Addressing the barriers faced by small businesses

01

Closing the financing gap

Many start-ups and small businesses could not access traditional bank loans because they lacked sufficient collateral or credit history. NEDCO was created to improve access to microfinance and business loans.

02

Empowering underserved groups

It promotes entrepreneurship and self-reliance among people who may face social or economic barriers, including young people, women and low-income earners.

03

Building business skills

NEDCO provides more than financing. Training, mentorship and business advice help entrepreneurs develop an idea and manage a business.

04

Diversifying the economy

By encouraging people to use local skills, talents and resources in different industries, NEDCO supports job creation and helps reduce dependence on the oil and gas sector.

02 / Mission & objectives

Helping small businesses move forward

NEDCO’s mission focuses on giving entrepreneurs the support and opportunities they need to start, manage and grow successful businesses in Trinidad and Tobago.

NEDCO’s mission

To help people start and develop small businesses by providing access to support, resources and opportunities that encourage financial independence and long-term business success.

The bigger goal: helping entrepreneurs build businesses that can stand on their own.
What guides its work

Main objectives

These objectives explain how NEDCO works towards its mission and contributes to the development of the small-business sector.

01

Promoting entrepreneurship

Encouraging more people to explore self-employment and turn useful ideas, skills and talents into business opportunities.

02

Providing financial assistance

Helping entrepreneurs access funding to establish a business, cover approved operating needs or support expansion when traditional financing may be difficult to obtain.

03

Developing business skills

Building practical knowledge in areas such as business planning, management, budgeting, recordkeeping and decision-making.

04

Supporting business growth

Assisting existing micro and small businesses to improve their operations, address challenges, reach customers and become more competitive.

05

Encouraging economic development

Supporting the creation of businesses, employment and income while strengthening local enterprise and contributing to a more diverse economy.

The challenges behind the mission

Problems NEDCO seeks to address

Starting and maintaining a business can be difficult, especially when entrepreneurs do not have the money, experience or guidance they need.

01

Limited access to money

Some business owners struggle to secure enough funding to get started, purchase what they need or expand their operations.

02

Lack of business experience

New entrepreneurs may have strong ideas but limited experience in planning, managing finances or running daily operations.

03

Limited advice and guidance

Without reliable business advice, owners may find it difficult to make informed decisions or solve problems as they arise.

04

Difficulty growing a business

Existing businesses can struggle to improve their services, compete in the market or maintain steady growth over time.

Why this matters: By addressing these barriers through financing and business-development support, NEDCO aims to give more entrepreneurs a fair opportunity to establish and sustain their businesses.
03 / Services

Four ways NEDCO supports entrepreneurs

NEDCO offers more than financial assistance. Its services are designed to help entrepreneurs develop an idea, manage a business and work towards long-term growth.

01Financial support

Business financing and loans

NEDCO provides financing to eligible entrepreneurs who need capital to start, operate or expand a business. Depending on the approved purpose, funds may help with purchasing equipment, stock, vehicles or other resources needed for business operations.

Why it mattersIt can provide another financing option for small businesses that may find it difficult to qualify for loans from traditional commercial banks.
02Skills development

Business training and workshops

Training sessions and workshops help entrepreneurs build the practical skills needed to run a business. Topics may include financial management, marketing, business planning and other areas of business operations.

Why it mattersBusiness owners can improve their knowledge, make more informed decisions and manage their resources more effectively.
03Professional guidance

Business advisory services

NEDCO provides advice to people who are preparing to start a business as well as those who already operate one. Guidance may include identifying business challenges, developing business plans, improving marketing approaches and managing finances.

Why it mattersHaving access to guidance can help entrepreneurs understand their options, address problems and make stronger business decisions.
04Growth and mentorship

Mentorship and business development

Through initiatives such as the Business Accelerator Programme (BAP), entrepreneurs can receive development support to help set goals, strengthen products or services and plan for business growth. Such programmes can also create opportunities for networking and further business support.

Why it mattersMentorship can help business owners apply what they learn, build confidence and work towards sustainable growth.
Important: Financing approval, programme entry and available services depend on NEDCO’s current requirements and offerings. Entrepreneurs should confirm details directly with NEDCO.

05 / Supporting entrepreneurship

Turning barriers into steps forward

NEDCO supports entrepreneurship by helping people overcome some of the common difficulties involved in starting and operating a business. Its assistance combines access to finance with the knowledge and guidance entrepreneurs need to make progress.

01Financial opportunity

Improving access to capital

One of the biggest challenges for a new or small business is finding enough money to begin operations or meet business needs. NEDCO's financing services can help eligible entrepreneurs obtain funds for approved expenses such as equipment, stock and other resources.

How this helpsBusinesses may be better able to get started, maintain operations and invest in areas that support their development.
02Knowledge and skills

Building stronger business skills

Having a good business idea is only the beginning. Through training and workshops, entrepreneurs can develop skills in planning, budgeting, recordkeeping, costing, marketing and management. These skills help owners understand how to organise and run their businesses.

How this helpsBusiness owners can make more informed decisions, manage resources responsibly and avoid some common management mistakes.
03Advice and mentorship

Providing guidance and confidence

Entrepreneurs may face problems they do not know how to solve on their own. NEDCO's advisory and mentorship support can help them review business plans, identify weaknesses, consider possible solutions and set realistic goals for improvement.

How this helpsAccess to guidance can make business owners feel better prepared to deal with challenges and make decisions about the future of their businesses.
04Long-term development

Encouraging growth and formalisation

As a business develops, its owner may need to improve daily operations, serve more customers, increase productivity or prepare for expansion. NEDCO's range of support can help entrepreneurs strengthen the way their businesses operate and understand the steps involved in developing further.

How this helpsStronger operations can help businesses become more organised, competitive and prepared for future opportunities.
05
The bigger picture

Support that goes beyond a loan

NEDCO's approach recognises that money alone does not guarantee business success. Entrepreneurs also need practical knowledge, sound planning and ongoing guidance. By bringing these forms of assistance together, NEDCO aims to help micro and small businesses become more capable of surviving and growing.

06 / Accessing the organisation's services

How entrepreneurs access NEDCO's services

Entrepreneurs can access NEDCO through online applications and bookings, as well as in-person consultations at its regional branches. The process helps applicants identify suitable services and understand the requirements before proceeding.

06
Ways to access support

Online or in person

Through NEDCO's official website, entrepreneurs can find information and access available booking, registration and application services. Applicants may also meet with a Business Relationship Officer at a regional branch to discuss their needs, eligibility and possible financing or training options.

The four-step loan process
01
Initial consultation

Meet with an officer

The entrepreneur discusses the business idea or existing business with a NEDCO officer and explores the available options.

02
Application

Submit documents

The applicant submits the loan application together with the supporting documents required by NEDCO.

03
Assessment

Financial and business review

NEDCO assesses the applicant's financial position and evaluates the business plan to determine whether the proposal meets its requirements.

04
Decision and support

Approval and disbursement

If approved, funds are disbursed according to NEDCO's procedures, including direct payment to suppliers where applicable. Entrepreneurs may also receive ongoing mentorship.

Eligibility criteria
01Nationality and age

Who can apply?

Applicants must be citizens or legal residents of Trinidad and Tobago and be at least 18 years old.

02Business type

Eligible enterprises

Applicants must operate or plan to establish a Micro or Small Enterprise (MSE) in a legal industry. The supplied criteria identify primary agriculture, gambling and alcohol retail as excluded activities.

03Financial standing

Access to finance

NEDCO targets entrepreneurs whose financing needs may not be met by traditional commercial banks. Applicants must also have no pending criminal court proceedings, according to the supplied criteria.

Application and registration requirements
01Personal identification

Identification documents

Two forms of valid Trinidad and Tobago government-issued identification and a personal Board of Inland Revenue (BIR) number.

02Business documentation

Business information

Certificate of Registration, business BIR number, proof of business address and proof of operations for existing businesses. Certain start-up programmes may accept applicants before registration, but formal registration may be required before funds are disbursed.

03Financial records

Plans and projections

A viable business plan, cash-flow projections and supplier quotations for equipment or stock purchases may be required.

Free and paid services

Free or subsidised services

Initial business assessments, advisory consultations, mentorship, Business Accelerator Programmes and certain government-administered grants may be offered at no cost. The supplied information identifies a Micro and Small Business Grant of up to TT$20,000, which does not require repayment, subject to programme rules and availability.

Paid or repayable services

Small-business loans must be repaid. The supplied information lists an 8% fixed interest rate and repayment terms of up to five years, with borrowing limits stated as up to TT$250,000 for first-time borrowers and TT$500,000 for repeat borrowers. Some specialised short courses may also have registration fees.

07 / Impact on business development

Contributing to national and regional economic development

Established in 2002 by the Government of Trinidad and Tobago, NEDCO supports the development of micro and small enterprises and contributes to national entrepreneurship goals. Its work can influence business growth, employment and the wider economy.

01Economic diversification

Expanding beyond energy

NEDCO can help reduce Trinidad and Tobago's reliance on the energy sector, including oil and gas, by supporting businesses in non-energy areas such as retail, light manufacturing, services and creative industries.

Potential contributionA wider range of productive industries and business opportunities.
02Employment and livelihoods

Job creation and poverty reduction

By helping people who face social or economic barriers to establish businesses, NEDCO can encourage self-employment, create jobs and increase economic activity and spending within local communities.

Potential contributionMore opportunities for people to earn an income and participate in the economy.
03Regional development

Caribbean regional benchmarking

NEDCO's state-backed microfinance and enterprise-development approach can serve as a regional example of how integrated public support may help connect informal micro-enterprises with the formal business sector and commercial finance.

Potential contributionA model for combining financing, training and enterprise guidance within the Caribbean.
Key contributions to entrepreneurial development
01Micro-financing and grants

Closing the financing gap

Traditional commercial banks may turn away start-ups and micro-entrepreneurs who lack collateral or an established credit history. NEDCO's loans and eligible non-repayable grants can provide capital for inventory, equipment and other approved business needs. The supplied information also refers to a possible grace period of up to three months, subject to loan conditions.

How this helpsEntrepreneurs may gain access to the resources needed to start, maintain or expand their operations.
02Training and formalisation

Building business capacity

NEDCO combines financing with entrepreneurial training, business-plan preparation and one-on-one mentorship. The supplied information describes targeted short courses of approximately six hours. This support can help entrepreneurs improve their management skills and move informal operations towards formal registration and tax compliance.

How this helpsBusiness owners can become more organised, informed and prepared to manage their enterprises.
03Innovation and market access

Developing ideas through the Business Accelerator Programme

Through partnerships with technical and educational institutions, NEDCO can help early-stage entrepreneurs test products with focus groups, improve their operations and prepare innovative ideas for the market.

How this helpsEntrepreneurs can refine products and services before taking them to a wider market.
07
Overall impact

Supporting businesses can strengthen the wider economy

By helping entrepreneurs access finance, develop their skills and improve their business ideas, NEDCO can contribute to stronger enterprises, employment opportunities and a more diverse economy. These outcomes are influenced by business performance and wider economic conditions, so support does not guarantee success.

08 / Challenges & limitations

Understanding the limitations

Although NEDCO provides important support to entrepreneurs, it cannot remove every difficulty involved in running a business. There are limitations that can affect both the organisation's services and the businesses receiving assistance.

01Financial responsibility

Loan repayment difficulties

Businesses may experience slow sales, unexpected expenses or changes in customer demand. These challenges can make it difficult to earn enough money to meet loan repayments on time.

Why it mattersBorrowing creates a financial responsibility, so entrepreneurs need realistic budgets and a clear plan for repayment.
02Availability of support

Limited resources

NEDCO has to work within the resources available to it. The demand for loans, training and business advice may be greater than the support that can be provided at a particular time.

Why it mattersNot every entrepreneur may receive assistance immediately, and some services may have limited availability.
03Application process

Eligibility and approval requirements

Applicants must meet the relevant requirements for the service or programme they are seeking. A business may not qualify for a particular loan or initiative, and approval is not guaranteed.

Why it mattersEntrepreneurs need to understand the criteria and prepare the necessary information before applying.
04External factors

Competition and changing market conditions

Even with financing and guidance, businesses must deal with competition, rising operating costs, inflation and changes in what customers want. These factors are not fully within NEDCO's control.

Why it mattersEntrepreneurs must continue adapting, monitoring their market and making sound business decisions.
08
A balanced view

Support improves opportunities, but does not guarantee success

NEDCO can provide useful resources and guidance, but entrepreneurs still have to manage their businesses responsibly and respond to challenges. The effectiveness of support also depends on the needs of each business and the wider economic environment.

These points are a general analysis of possible challenges and limitations, not an official NEDCO-published list.

09 / Conclusion

Why NEDCO matters to entrepreneurship

NEDCO plays an important role in helping entrepreneurs turn business ideas into real opportunities by combining financial assistance with practical business support.

NEDCO is vital to entrepreneurship because it provides accessible loans, business training and mentorship to start-ups and small businesses that may struggle to obtain funding from traditional banks. By lowering financial barriers and guiding new business owners, especially underserved groups such as youth, women and low-income earners, it can encourage self-employment, support job creation and contribute to economic diversification.

Entrepreneurs can benefit from NEDCO because its financing may offer an alternative to the stricter collateral requirements often associated with commercial banks. In addition to financial support, NEDCO provides practical training, mentorship and advisory services in areas such as bookkeeping and business planning. This combination of funding and guidance can help business owners manage their finances, avoid common early mistakes and improve their chances of building successful businesses.

Final point: NEDCO gives entrepreneurs access to resources and guidance that can make starting and developing a business more achievable, although long-term success still depends on careful planning, responsible decisions and consistent effort.
