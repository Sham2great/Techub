01 / Introduction

What is NEDCO?

NEDCO (National Entrepreneurship Development Company Limited) is a wholly state-owned enterprise (limited liability company) and microfinance development agency in Trinidad and Tobago.

About the organisation

It is completely owned by the Government of Trinidad and Tobago and operates under the oversight of the Ministry of Youth Development and National Service.

As a non-bank microfinance institution, NEDCO provides small loans to micro and small business owners who may have difficulty accessing financing from commercial banks, including entrepreneurs who may not meet traditional collateral requirements.

In addition to financing, NEDCO supports entrepreneurs through business training, mentorship and advisory services to help micro and small enterprises develop, grow and succeed.

ESTABLISHEDAugust 2002

NEDCO was established by the Government of Trinidad and Tobago as a state-owned, limited liability company to support the development of micro and small enterprises.

Why it was established

Addressing the barriers faced by small businesses

01

Closing the financing gap

Many start-ups and small businesses could not access traditional bank loans because they lacked sufficient collateral or credit history. NEDCO was created to improve access to microfinance and business loans.

02

Empowering underserved groups

It promotes entrepreneurship and self-reliance among people who may face social or economic barriers, including young people, women and low-income earners.

03

Building business skills

NEDCO provides more than financing. Training, mentorship and business advice help entrepreneurs develop an idea and manage a business.

04

Diversifying the economy

By encouraging people to use local skills, talents and resources in different industries, NEDCO supports job creation and helps reduce dependence on the oil and gas sector.

02 / Mission & objectives

Building capable businesses

Mission in brief

Support new and existing micro and small businesses so they can become financially independent, sustainable and competitive.

In simple terms: help businesses stand on their own.

Main objectives

  • Improve access to business financing.
  • Develop entrepreneurs’ knowledge and skills.
  • Support business planning and assessment.
  • Encourage sustainable growth.
Need addressed: Entrepreneurs may have a viable idea but lack funding, business knowledge, guidance or resources to establish a business.
03 / Services

More than just a loan

Business financing

Loan options for eligible registered businesses to support approved business needs.

Entrepreneurial training

Practical learning to strengthen business and management skills.

Business advisory

Guidance to help owners assess needs and make business decisions.

Business assessment

Assessment of business needs and readiness during development or financing.

Mentorship

Support to apply business knowledge and work through challenges.

Business Accelerator Programme

Development support that may combine training, advisory, mentorship, networking and financing access.

05 / Supporting entrepreneurship

Turning barriers into steps forward

Access to capital

Financing may help eligible businesses purchase stock, equipment or approved necessities.

Better business skills

Training can improve planning, recordkeeping, costing, marketing and management.

Guidance and confidence

Advisory and mentorship can help owners evaluate choices and solve problems.

Growth and formalisation

Support can strengthen operations and prepare businesses for expansion.

06 / Accessing services

How to get started

Contact NEDCO

Visit its official website or contact a branch to discuss the service needed.

Discuss the business

A Business Relationship Officer can discuss needs, viability and possible options.

Prepare requirements

Applicants should expect to provide business information and supporting documents; requirements vary by application.

Assessment

NEDCO reviews the application; approval depends on its current criteria.

Note: Financing is for registered businesses and approved business purposes, not personal spending; confirm eligibility, fees and loan terms directly with NEDCO.

07 / Impact

Why NEDCO matters

Encourages new businesses

Financing and guidance can help entrepreneurs move from idea to operation.

Strengthens small firms

Training and advice can improve management and competitiveness.

Supports the economy

Small businesses can contribute to employment, local production and economic diversity.

These are potential contributions of its services, not a claim that every supported business succeeds.

08 / Challenges & limitations

Support has limits

Loan repayment risk

Low sales or rising costs can make repayment difficult for businesses.

Limited resources

Demand for financing, training and advice may exceed available resources.

Eligibility requirements

Not every business or activity will qualify for every service.

Business conditions

Competition, inflation and changing demand affect success even with support.

These are analytical points for the presentation, not an official NEDCO-published list.

09 / Conclusion

Backing the people behind the business

NEDCO is important because it combines financing with training and business-development support, helping micro and small entrepreneurs build stronger, more sustainable businesses in Trinidad and Tobago.

Final point: entrepreneurs can gain resources, skills and guidance, but success still depends on planning, responsible decisions and consistent effort.
